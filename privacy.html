Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 October 2026

ReAction Camera does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs.

Your photos

Photos you capture are processed entirely on your device. Depending on what you do, a photo is either saved to your Photo Library (with your permission) or handed to a Shortcut you chose, inside Apple's Shortcuts app. The app itself never uploads your photos anywhere.

Shortcuts

When a photo is passed to a Shortcut, what happens next is governed by that Shortcut and by Apple's Shortcuts app — not by ReAction Camera. If one of your Shortcuts sends an image somewhere (for example, to another app or service), that action is yours, under your control.

Permissions

ReAction Camera asks for Camera access (to take photos), and may ask for Microphone access (for Live Photos and video) and Photo Library access (to save captures). These are used only for the feature you invoked and are never sent to us.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and collects no data from anyone, including children.
