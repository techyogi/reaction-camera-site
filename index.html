ReAction

Your Action button, reborn.

Lost the Action button on iPhone Air? ReAction brings it back on Camera Control: press to capture and fire any Shortcut — straight from the photo, no clipboard, no paste prompts.

What it does

  • One press, straight to your Shortcut. Press Camera Control, capture, and your Shortcut runs with the photo already in hand.
  • No paste prompts. The image is handed to Shortcuts directly as a typed file — nothing touches your clipboard.
  • Starter shortcuts built in. Quick Look, Remove Background, Extract Text, Open in Any App — add one in a tap.
  • Trigger it your way. Camera Control, Back Tap, the Lock Screen, Control Center, or Siri.
  • Unfolds for more. On iPhone Duo and iPad the preview and controls fill the larger display.

Requires iOS 26. Camera Control and Back Tap need a supported iPhone.
